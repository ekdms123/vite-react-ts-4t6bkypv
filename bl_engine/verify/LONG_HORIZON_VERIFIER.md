# Long-Horizon Verifier

Compare checkpoints across low pressure, conflict, memory, high emotion, reveal, aftermath, and return-to-ordinary segments.

Surface may vary. The verifier looks for survival of the focal character's attention habits, judgment logic, emotional avoidance, and concrete reality anchors. Humor may vanish under pressure. A late return to generic narrator cognition is a failure even when the early section was strong.

Memory is checked separately: an embodied past fragment that changes present meaning may pass; an extended biographical chronology that no longer changes present judgment is drift.
