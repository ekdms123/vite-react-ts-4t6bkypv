# BASE PROSE / ATTENTION PROFILE — source-grounded mechanism model

This profile records transferable mechanisms, not phrases, motifs, setting, or signatures.

## Strong current hypothesis

The deepest stable mechanism is **focal attention selection + local practical judgment**, not a quota of body/logistical detail.

- The focal mind repeatedly privileges what is immediately usable, costly, risky, irritating, bodily, spatial, or necessary.
- Concrete detail follows that selection: money, transport, food, medication, injury, work, objects, weather, smell, distance, and routine appear because they are part of current processing.
- Small actions and judgments often arrive before complete emotional explanation.
- Relationship meaning is frequently performed through labor, access, tending, waiting, money, proximity, touch, responsibility, and changed boundaries.
- Pressure may narrow perception and optional thought, but no universal sentence-length rule is supported.
- Aftermath/integration can temporarily expand meaning before concrete reality returns.
- Some concrete reality is allowed to remain non-instrumental when it is focal-owned; not every object or action is a planted device.

## Conditional rather than universal

- delayed emotional naming;
- syntactic compression under acute pressure;
- temporary lyric expansion;
- coarse colloquial texture;
- isolated short lines.

## Anti-caricature

Do not reduce the profile to vulgarity, short syntax, body detail, chores, or logistical object insertion. Those are surface symptoms without the selecting cognition that makes them functional.
