# PLAN / AFFORDANCE / AGENCY ARCHITECTURE PROFILE — source-grounded mechanism model

## Strong current hypothesis

A shared long arc can preserve global causality while changing protagonist, tone, local problem type, and agency structure.

- Once identity mystery is reduced, forward pull can migrate to hidden plans, strategic inference, remaining options, and what another agent is trying to make the protagonist do.
- Character agency is visible in how perceived options and strategy change, not merely in stronger declarations.
- Relationship change can transform joint action topology: what two people can now do together, what risks they share, and what permissions/obligations exist.
- Organizations and adversaries should adapt to each other's moves; strategic agents are not static obstacles.
- Large payoffs can produce multi-dimensional gain across knowledge, causality, institutions/resources, options, relationship, and future horizon.

## Anti-caricature

Do not turn this into constant plotting, chess metaphors, or every scene carrying a hidden master plan. Strategic ecology is activated only where material agents and consequences justify it.
