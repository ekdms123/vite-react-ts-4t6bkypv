## Strong integrated source relations

1. Attention must be selected before concrete style symptoms are realized.
2. Character cognition must precede humor surface.
3. Reader pull is a dynamic problem model, not a cliffhanger quota.
4. Relationship change is frequently an affordance/permission change, not an intimacy label.
5. Major reveal quality depends on preserved prior plausibility and forward consequence, but minor reveals require lighter handling.
6. High pressure changes accessible cognition but does not mandate one sentence shape.
7. Recurrence gains value from recoding; mere recognition is not payoff.
8. The same stable author identity may occupy different scene-conditioned style regions.

## Negative evidence / anti-caricature

Do not promote as universal author rules:

- short sentences;
- one-line paragraphs;
- vulgarity/coarseness;
- body-detail frequency targets;
- always-delayed emotion naming;
- always-on humor;
- bizarre comparison quotas;
- constant misdirection;
- every repeated object becoming motif;
- every scene producing a large state delta;
- every reveal becoming a retrospective architecture event.

## Holdout limit

The available holdout is not pristine because portions of the sources were already inspected during prior analysis. It may support regression consistency only. Blind literary superiority remains ASSURANCE_NOT_MET until a genuinely independent evaluation exists.
