# HUMOR / CHARACTER FRAME PROFILE — source-grounded mechanism model

## Strong current hypothesis

Humor is primarily a **character-owned frame transfer and inference process** rather than a library of funny comparisons.

- A character may map an unrelated conceptual system onto the current situation, then pursue that mapping with internally consistent logic.
- Register drops, banal collisions, awkward literalization, self-serving reframing, and late conversational turns are surface outcomes of that cognition.
- Different characters require different cognitive-frame repertoires; the same joke logic should not be copied across the cast.
- Humor may disappear under pressure without counting as identity loss.
- Humor cannot dictate paragraph topology, POV distance, or story causality.

## Secondary transferable value

Memory and knowledge asymmetry are important beyond humor. Stored knowledge, current activation, reconstructed memory, and what a character pretends to remember may diverge.

## Anti-caricature

Do not force unusual comparisons, register drops, or punchlines at a fixed frequency. Repeated use of the same frame-transfer operation becomes caricature.
