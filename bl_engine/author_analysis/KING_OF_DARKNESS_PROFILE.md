# READER HYPOTHESIS / EVIDENCE ARCHITECTURE PROFILE — source-grounded mechanism model

## Strong current hypothesis

The story engine is better described as **dynamic reader problem-model management** than generic mystery/twist design.

- Several explanatory models can remain plausible at once.
- Earlier models should be rational from visible evidence rather than merely wrong.
- Evidence has provenance: perception, testimony, memory, inference, rumor, and deception are not identical to truth.
- New evidence can change relative plausibility before any final answer is given.
- Major reveals can shift ontology, identity, causal interpretation, or character meaning rather than merely replacing one suspect with another.
- Retrospective reconstruction should make earlier behavior more intelligible without invalidating the evidence that made the earlier model possible.
- Old clues may require reactivation before payoff; technical prior existence alone is not sufficient fairness.

## Reveal routing

Distinguish at least:

- local deductive reveal;
- consequential causal reveal;
- identity/character reveal;
- ontological reinterpretation.

Do not impose one fairness/accounting rule on all reveal types.
